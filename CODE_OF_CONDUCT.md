# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming and
inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive
criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments,
and publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a
GitHub issue or contacting the maintainers directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated.
